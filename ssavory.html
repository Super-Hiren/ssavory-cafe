About Ssavory

More than just a meal.

Ssavory is a restro and cafe in Mota Varachha, built for evenings that don't need to end quickly.

The menu travels from sizzlers and pizzas to slow paneer curries, Chinese starters and Thai bowls, all of it vegetarian. Guests regularly mention the food, the service and the atmosphere in the same breath.

Inside, arched windows, curved banquettes and planters between the tables make the room feel calm. Soft music and warm lighting set the pace, whether you're here for a date night, a family dinner or an iced latte with a friend.

  • All-vegetarian menu
  • Warm lighting, soft music
  • Date nights and family dinners
  • Cafe drinks all day
